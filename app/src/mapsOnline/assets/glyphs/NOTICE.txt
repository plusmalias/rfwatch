Map label glyphs (Noto Sans Bold, Unicode range 0-255) in MapLibre/Mapbox glyph PBF format,
as published by OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org/fonts). Noto Sans is licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). Bundled so map labels ("YOU",
cluster counts) render without contacting a font server.
